# Legal / privacy readiness — MVP 0.1

## Implemented technical controls

- user data export,
- account deletion,
- audit records for sensitive operations,
- private upload storage in the current local-storage architecture,
- sensitive analytics-property filtering,
- public test-version privacy and terms information pages,
- no employer-side candidate ranking in the current roadmap,
- no matching based on protected/sensitive traits by product design.

## External information required before public beta

The repository cannot truthfully complete a final Polish privacy policy or terms without business/legal facts that are not present in the implementation specification. At minimum the release owner must provide and approve:

1. legal name / business name of the controller and service provider,
2. registered/contact address and contact channel,
3. final purposes and legal bases for each processing activity,
4. final processor/subprocessor list (hosting, AI, analytics, error monitoring, email, payments, storage),
5. international-transfer safeguards where applicable,
6. retention periods and deletion schedule,
7. data-subject request process,
8. cookie/local-storage inventory and consent classification,
9. final subscription/payment, withdrawal, complaint and refund rules,
10. legal review for the Polish/EU launch context.

Until those items are supplied and approved, `public/privacy.html` and `public/terms.html` deliberately identify themselves as test-version information, not final legal documents.
